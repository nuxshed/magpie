Privacy

Magpie is a personal board app. It has no server that stores your data.

Your boards and images are saved in your browser and in a Magpie folder in your own Google Drive. Magpie uses the drive.file permission, so it can only see files it created, nothing else in your Drive.

When you add a link, its address is sent to a small Cloudflare Worker that fetches the page title and preview image. Nothing about you is logged or kept.

There are no analytics, ads or tracking. To remove everything, delete the Magpie folder in your Drive and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
